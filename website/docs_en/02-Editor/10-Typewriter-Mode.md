# Typewriter Mode

Typewriter mode keeps **the cursor vertically centered on screen at all times**. As you keep typing, the text scrolls up while the line you're on stays pinned to the middle — just like an old typewriter, locking your attention onto the current line.

> [!NOTE]
> Shortcut: `Ctrl+Alt+T` to toggle; you can also leave it always on via the "Appearance" group in [[07-Settings/01-General-Settings]].

## What It Solves

- **Less visual fatigue**: your eyes don't have to jump between the top and the middle of the screen
- **Sustained focus**: the current line is always centered, while surrounding text naturally falls out of view
- **No more manual scrolling**: when writing long documents, you don't need to reach for the scroll wheel

## How to Enable

1. Press `Ctrl+Alt+T` to toggle typewriter mode immediately; or
2. Open Settings (`Ctrl+,`) → "General" tab → "Appearance" group → turn on the "Typewriter mode" toggle.

> [!TIP]
> Typewriter mode is compatible with both "live preview" and "source" editing modes and can be layered on top of either; it also works with Vim mode enabled.

## When to Use It

- Long-form writing, essays, journals
- Focused tasks that need a flow state
- Writers who prefer a centered cursor
